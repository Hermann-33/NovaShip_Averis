# Shipping documentation glossary

- SI (Shipping Instruction): the shipper's instruction to the carrier describing the cargo and parties. Labels seen on documents: "Shipping Instruction", "BL Instruction", "Bill of Lading Instruction".
- Draft BL (Draft Bill of Lading): the carrier's pre-issue version of the Bill of Lading for checking. Labels: "Bill of Lading (Draft)", "Draft BL", "B/L No.".
- Shipper: the exporter. Labels: "Shipper", "Shipper/Exporter", "Shipper (Principal or Seller)", "Exporter".
- Consignee: the receiver of the goods. Labels: "Consignee", "Consignee (Non-Negotiable)", "To the Order of".
- Notify Party: the party to be notified on arrival. Labels: "Notify", "Notify Party", "Notify Party/Intermediate Consignee". It is a comparison field, not a permission to send.
- Port of Loading (POL): where cargo is loaded. Labels: "Load Port", "Port of Loading (POL)", "POL".
- Port of Discharge (POD): where cargo is unloaded. Labels: "Discharge Port", "Port of Discharge (POD)", "POD".
- Container Count: number of containers, e.g. "6 x 40'HC" means six 40-foot high-cube containers. Labels: "No. of Containers", "Total Containers", "Container Count", "No. of Containers or Packages".
- Gross Weight (kg): total weight including packaging. Labels: "Gross Weight (KG)", "Gross Wt (kgs)", "GROSS WEIGHT". MT means metric tonnes: 1 MT = 1000 kg.
- OC number: APRIL order confirmation reference, pattern 5XXX-00000 (e.g. 5RSG-00133).
- Booking reference: carrier booking number (e.g. MSDUL0942518196 for MSC, ONEYSINF32871 for ONE).
- Telex release / Surrender BL: the original BL is surrendered at origin so cargo can be released at destination without paper originals.
- THC: terminal handling charge. D&D: demurrage and detention charges. GR: goods receipt (SAP) needed before an invoice can be billed.
- SWB: sea waybill. OBL: original bill of lading. LC: letter of credit. CFR / FOB: Incoterms.
